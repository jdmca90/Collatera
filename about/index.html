About Collatera

A clinical reference project, built alongside fellowship

Collatera began as a personal reference library — a way to keep clinical images, society guidelines, and practical notes gathered during cardiology fellowship in one place I could actually find again.

The name nods to collateral circulation: alternate pathways that keep tissue perfused when the primary route is blocked.

It felt like a fitting metaphor for something built quietly alongside formal training. What started as a private study tool is growing into something meant to be shared — a resource hub built for peers, for fellows and colleagues who could use the same kind of organized, at-a-glance reference during training and beyond.

What's here

  • Reference Image Library Algorithms, anatomy, and clinical images, searchable and tagged.
  • Guideline Repository Direct links to society guidelines, sorted by topic, society, and year.
  • Slide Decks Presentations and talks, readable in the browser.
  • How To Guides Walkthroughs for the things nobody quite teaches you.
  • Self-Education Hub Vetted outside resources worth your time.

Built for fellows, residents, and colleagues in cardiology — and anyone who has ever lost a useful figure in a camera roll.
